UX Research & Strategy Master Grading Handbook (Instructor Edition) Internal document for instructors and teaching assistants. Purpose: ensure consistent evaluation, fast grading, and calibrated expectations across all course assignments. 1. Course Evaluation Philosophy The course uses a studio pipeline model. Each assignment builds on previous artifacts and contributes to a final product concept. Students are evaluated primarily on: 1. Quality of reasoning 2. Evidence of synthesis 3. Ability to apply UX frameworks 4. Iteration across assignments Visual polish alone should never determine grade level. Strong submissions demonstrate that the student understands why a design decision matters. 2. The Artifact Pipeline Assignments build progressively. Stage | Assignment | Focus Discovery | Persona | Understanding the user Context | Journey Map | Understanding the experience ecosystem Concept | Paper Prototype | Designing interaction flows Structure | Wireframes | Interface architecture Critique | Aesthetic Analysis | Visual design reasoning Evaluation | Heuristic Evaluation | Usability analysis Instructors should check continuity between artifacts. Example: Persona > Journey > Prototype > Wireframe decisions If assignments appear disconnected, the work likely lacks synthesis. 3. Performance Scale Score | Interpretation 4.0 | Demonstrates synthesis, strong reasoning, and clear framework use 3.7-3.9 | Very strong work with minor gaps 3.3-3.6 | Competent work but limited depth 2.7-3.2 | Partial understanding 2.0-2.6 | Significant conceptual gaps Below 2.0 | Incomplete or fundamentally flawed 4. Instructor Fast-Grading Method To grade efficiently, instructors should evaluate submissions using three questions: 1. Does the artifact clearly demonstrate the assignment objective? 2. Does it show synthesis rather than description? 3. Does it connect to previous artifacts? If the answer to all three is yes = 4.0 range. If one dimension is weak = 3.x range. If multiple dimensions are weak = 2.x range. 5. Grading Decision Tree Use this when determining the final score. Step 1 - Artifact Completion Does the submission include all required elements? If no = cap at 3.0 Step 2 - Design Reasoning Does the student explain why their design decisions matter? If reasoning is minimal = 3.x or below Step 3 - Framework Application Does the work correctly apply the course frameworks? If frameworks are missing or misapplied = 3.x Step 4 - Insight Quality Does the work show original insight or synthesis? If yes = 4.0 candidate 6.

Rapid Grading Template (90-Second Method) Each assignment can be evaluated with the following template. Instructor notes format: Strengths: ... Opportunities: ... Score: X.X Typical feedback length should be 3-6 sentences. This keeps grading scalable for large classes. 7. AI-Assisted Work Detection AI assistance is permitted for writing support, but students must demonstrate synthesis. Indicators of over-templated or AI-generated work include: Linguistic Signals: - Perfect grammar but vague ideas - Generic phrases repeated across submissions - Lack of product-specific detail Example: Weak AI-style statement: Users value efficiency and want a seamless experience. Stronger student reasoning: Students commuting to campus need to complete tasks quickly between classes, which creates pressure for simplified navigation. Structural Signals: AI-heavy submissions often show: - overly symmetrical formatting - identical paragraph length - no concrete examples Required Instructor Annotation if detected: "Reads as technically perfect but low on original synthesis (possible AI or over-templated phrasing)." TA Calibration Process: Before grading begins, instructors should: 1. Review 3 example submissions 2. Assign preliminary scores 3. Compare grading decisions 4. Align on rubric interpretation This prevents grade drift. Recommended Grading Workflow: For each assignment: 1. Skim submission (20 seconds) 2. Identify strongest insight 3. Identify major gap 4. Assign score using decision tree 5. Write 3-5 sentence feedback Target grading time: 60-90 seconds per submission Focus feedback on: reasoning, synthesis, connection between artifacts. Avoid focusing primarily on: visual polish, formatting. Students should leave the course understanding how UX professionals reason about design problems.

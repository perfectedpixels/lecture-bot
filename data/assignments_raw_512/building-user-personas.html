Create two Personas; The Loyal Foodie and The Distracted Urbanite that best describe the ideal customer who would be interested in an online delivery service offered by Trader Joe's, an artisan grocery chain. You can also consider in-store innovations as well. 

Trader Joe's Business Goals for the online experience is to:

  • Gain a new customer segment that can be enticed by online shopping, but still stays true to its brand
  • Increase frequency of customers purchasing by optimizing for user needs
  • Increase the average quantity of items purchased by hyper-efficient suggestion model and ease of use
  • Inspire customers to experiment with new foods
  • Create a unique branded experience that is authentic to Trader Joe's
  • Target their identified audiences: Loyal Foodie & Distracted Urbanite.
    • The Distracted Urbanite enjoys the convenience of the ready made and easy to cook, with a small number of cart items.
    • The Loyal Foodie They loyally shop for supplemental items for their family seeking out items that are unique to Trader Joe's that they can't get from their regular grocery shopping, with a larger cart of items.

For each personas, you will need to:

  • Visit a Trader Joe's and observe customers. Take notes to identify trends:
    • How do they dress?
    • How old are they?
    • What kinds of things are they buying?
    • What are they driving (or not)?
    • Who are they with when they are shopping?
      • What are they talking about?
    • Are there similarities between any kind of demographics?
    • What is the brand connection with the persona?
    • What are their food habits and shopping cart size and food choices?
    • What type of grocery or recipe related apps are they using, if any?
    • How long do they stay in the store? Do they meander or hone in on specific things?

  • For each persona, your should:

    • What to look for:
      • Identify consistent issues 
      • Identify patterns of behavior
      • Figure out which issues you are going to tackle
      • Keep track of insights and ideas
    • From your observations, identify the trends and demographics you noticed and try to bucket which ones meet the general audiences as you have identified them. Do further online research on any supplemental demographic data, research or associated articles can be found that overlaps with your identified users. Any empirical research that helps round out your understanding can be used.
    • If you find that the differences break differently than by generation and are based on some other factor (ideology, locale, socioeconomic conditions, etc.) you are welcome to change the criteria of your personas to reflect that.
    • Find a photo to represent your target Personas and give them names.
    • Include a two paragraph narrative to represent their daily routine when it comes to food at home, and the differentiators of each persona and their relationship to Trader Joe's within that routine. Avoid “mini short stories” that over-personalize a persona, losing elasticity and representativeness. In professional UX practice, the persona narrative should describe archetypal behavior patterns, not singular life events. A well-written day-in-the-life vignette should illuminate repeatable motivations and contextual triggers, not idiosyncratic routines.
    • Include Demographics (marital status, household size and income, age, etc.) and associated interests: Favorite TV Show, Car they Drive, Hobby, job title, how often they spend time food shopping, cooking food or watching a TV.
    • Based on your research, provide additional statistics that you identify about your persona's makeup like political affiliation, race breakdown, suburban vs urban ratio, discretionary income spending, largest and smallest concerns, etc.
    • Write 4 User Pain Points / User Needs and the solutions Trader Joe's can provide, comprising of in-store and online that support the hypothesis in your findings and from the articles. Make sure they speak to Trader Joe's, and not just generic grocery shopping and are specific to each persona.
    • List up to 5 Content, or Services that are most important to them on a daily basis (Facetiming, reading news articles, hustling for cash, binge watching, health checks, exercising, etc.)
    • Include three references for additional resources of where you made your decision about this user. As long as you seek one data source that describes that Persona, you should be on the right track. 
    • You are not required to make it look designed 

     

  • Grading Rubric (4.0 Scale)

    Category 4.0 (Excellent) 3.0 (Proficient) 2.0 (Developing) 1.0 (Needs Work)
    Field Observation & Patterns (20%) Clear, specific, and pattern-based; directly tied to persona traits and insights. Some concrete notes; partial link to insights. Minimal or generic notes; unclear link to personas. Missing or vague field data.
    Research Integration (15%) Multiple credible sources support demographics and behaviors; cited correctly. Some data used; limited citation depth. Minimal or outdated research; unclear sources. No supporting research.
    Persona Clarity & Differentiation(20%) Personas are vivid, distinct, realistic, and non-stereotyped. Clear personas with some overlap or simplification. Basic demographic sketches; limited differentiation. Unclear or implausible personas.
    Narrative & Brand Connection(15%) Strong daily routine and Trader Joe’s relationship; authentic to brand tone. Routine described but weak  Basic description; mostly generic grocery context. Missing or off-brand.
    Pain Points & TJ-Specific Solutions(20%) Four clear needs with actionable, on-brand solutions (online & in-store). Needs identified; partial relevance. Generic or weakly linked solutions. Few or missing needs; no clear solutions.

Describe what you see as the main issues with the existing grading process / UI. Prioritize the issues and provide recommendations for how best to solve them.

Amazon reviews all products that are returned to determine the quality level of each item before it is re-sold under the Warehouse Deals name. Your assignment is to review how the Grading Associate interacts with the grading software and to determine what issues around the UI or procedures hamper quality or yield: 

  1. Review all the observation videos posted below
  2. Write down what you see as the key pain points and the negative outcomes that come from those issues
  3. Provide Recommendations that help solve for the key issues identified
  4. Based on viewing all the videos, use the two categories below, plus determine your own third Category (as the example below represents).
  5. For each category, complete 5 to 8 findings, outcomes, and solutions per category. I have included a sample entry for the first category. For any questions about the assignment or the videos, please post at the Q&A Class Assignments Discussion Board. 

Thoughtful consideration of the following will determine your grade:

  • What would improve efficiency?
  • How do you solve for accuracy issues?
  • What is missing from the existing process that you would recommend?
  • What changes would improve the overall customer experience?

Rubric

Criterion What It Assesses Weight
1. Insight & Systems Thinking How well the student interprets the observed behaviors or process breakdowns. Looks for nuanced understanding of why issues happen (human, systemic, incentive, or data causes). 1.2 pts
2. Relevance & Prioritization of Issues Whether the issues identified are meaningful and high-impact vs surface-level. Prioritization should reflect real UX judgment (customer, business, operations). 1.0 pt
3. Quality of Recommendations Do proposed fixes show design maturity — balancing feasibility, scale, and ethical/user implications? Goes beyond “add a button” to “change how performance is defined.” 1.2 pts
4. Clarity & Communication Is it concise, coherent, and persuasive? Would this hold up in a stakeholder readout? 0.6 pt

Total: 4.0 pts

  • 4.0 (Excellent): Sophisticated systems thinking; clear link from human problem → organizational cause → design or policy remedy. Shows empathy for multiple stakeholders (employees, customers, management).

  • 3.0–3.5 (Proficient): Clear logic and actionable recs but lacks some strategic depth or misses secondary implications.

  • 2.0–2.5 (Developing): Surface observations or generic solutions (“improve UI,” “train employees”).

  • 1.0–1.5 (Beginning): Limited insight; mainly descriptive or repeating known examples.

Imagine you are hired by Buddy TV (a TV news site to follow television shows) to build a fan site for a cult-following show. Buddy TV wants a sub-site that is compelling and unique to that show. Your objective is to uncover a unique set of offerings for BuddyTV that serves the show’s dedicated fans, increases user engagement with the content you create, and leverages social media.

You will be placed into a team, assigned to one of the cult-following shows below:

  • Kpop Demon Hunters
  • Stranger Things
  • Queer Eye
  • Squid Game
  • The Voice

Follow these steps:

  1. Individually, research the subject matter for the show you are assigned.
  2. Write a list of interview questions (approx. 10 questions per person) that uncover more about your target audience, using the techniques outlined in our class discussion. They are also available to reference in the slides here. UX Research Week 6.pdf
  3. As a group, interview 2-3 people that are fans of that show and discuss why the show is interesting to them. It is up to you to find those fans- they can even be other students from the class not in your group. Your Interview goal is to find out what a typical fan would want as information, entertainment, and content to support their interest in the show. Uncover what makes the show compelling and what kind of content BuddyTV should build for their fansite.
  4. Based on those learnings, come up with what offerings and features you would recommend for that audience that are contextual to that audience.
  5. Brainstorm and sketch ideas (one per team member), that you feel would be compelling for fans of that show, based on what you heard in your interviews, writing a 1-2 sentence justification how the idea is based on your users.

 

Based on your interview, your presentation includes:

  • 1. Audience Proto-Persona (1 slide). What are the types of interests your interviewees are interested in that relate to the topic/genre/culture of the show, as well as the age group you see would be that target audience.
  • 2. Product Objective (1 to 2 slides)
    • What is the core opportunity? Describe what would be valuable to this fan base that would deliver higher engagement with Buddy TV.
    • Why is it important to your customer? Explain what it is about your user experience that is specific to that fan community.
  • 3. Proposal of 1 applets / featurettes / games / contests / etc. for the fan site per team member. Consider ways to build momentum for your show through social media-driven marketing or user-generated content. Include:
    • Screens of the proposed experience as either snapshots of your whiteboard sketches, paper sketches, or low fidelity mockups using Google Slides, PowerPoint, Figma, Sketch, or Balsamiq. You can also leverage an LLM to improve the fidelity of your idea, but include the original sketch if you do.
    • Short explanation for each concept and justification based on the interviews
    • For each, explain why your proposed features would be valuable to this audience and how it maps back to your Product Objective
  • 4. Your individual Contributions
    • Research found
    • Interview questions
    • Interview Notes 

Rubric

Criteria 4.0 (Exceeds) 3.7 (Strong) 3.3 (Meets) 3.0 (Below)
Interview Quality(25%) Interview questions are open-ended, neutral, and dig into whyfans care. Notes show active listening and genuine insight. Questions are clear and conversational but probe lightly. Some missed opportunities to ask “why.” Questions gather useful info but stay surface-level. Little evidence of deeper probing. Questions are leading or minimal; limited insight gained.
Superfan Focus & Opportunity(20%) Targets true superfans; defines a clear, insight-driven opportunity based on what motivates them. Focused on relevant fans with mostly clear opportunity framing. Audience identified but opportunity feels general or assumption-based. Weak connection to fan motivations; unclear or missing opportunity.
Product Objective(15%) States what Buddy TV can uniquely offer and why it matters to this audience. Connects to fan behaviors and goals. Clear objective but not fully tied to user evidence or lacks specificity. Objective present but broad or generic. Objective unclear or unrelated to research.
Concept Idea & Justification(25%) One concept per member; each idea clearly links to interview insights and the Product Objective; creative and show-specific. Concept is thoughtful and connected but could be more tightly grounded in insights. Idea fits assignment but feels generic or under-explained. Concept missing or not based on user findings.
Persona & Presentation Quality(10%) Persona reflects culture and habits of the fan base; slides are cohesive and polished. Persona and slides clear but not deeply tied to insights. Persona surface-level; presentation uneven. Missing persona or incomplete presentation.
Scenario Thinking(5%) Demonstrates awareness of how fans would use the feature (happy and edge cases). Mentions typical use; light edge-case awareness. Touches on flow but lacks depth. No scenario thinking shown.

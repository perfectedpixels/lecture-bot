15-20 minute Summary Presentation 
The Summary Presentation deliverable makes up half of the grade you receive for the final. Make sure you have enough content for a twenty-minute presentation. Each member of the team needs to contribute to the presentation and you will need to share your time each presenting a portion of it. Prepare/Practice your final presentation inclusive of Jason/peer feedback as if you were doing a product review with a prospective VC for funding.

For your presentation:

  • Summarize in 22 minutes max (we will be timing) your project in Google, PowerPoint or Keynote and present:
    • final product pitch idea,
    • persona,
    • an overview of the main customer journey(s)
    • full end to end experience
  • Demo your hi-fi prototype from a user’s POV and tell it like a narrative highlighting onboarding, core navigation, backstack architecture, support along the way, happy and hard paths etc.
  • Summarize what you learned from user feedback that reveal what you learned along the way (include customer quotes and/or user feedback video).
  • Show what you changed
  • Summarize if you did it all over again what you would do differently. 
  • Have fun!

The goal of this interview script is to write down a number of questions to validate your ideas and to generate important insights to improve it. It doesn't need to be perfect, but should cover the main concerns you have about whether your concept provides value to the customer and meets their needs. You will use this script on with others in your class to receive their feedback, but you are welcome to use it on others prior. You should prepare no more than 10 questions.

Prepare your interview script to include the following: 

  • Questions that help refine or verify your assumptions for your persona. 
  • Questions that are based on what you heard in the class critique exercise. 
  • At least one IDEO user research technique that is most appropriate for your project and user. Go through the IDEO Method Cards.

Actions 

  • Author a script for receiving Feedback for a prototype walk-through that at least covers these specifics: 
  • Track accuracy of user expectations: For example, "From this page, how would you..."  or "From this screen, what do you think this service/application is for?" 
  • Usability of prototype: For example, "From a star rating of 1-5, 5 being best, how easy would you rate this experience"  
  • Value and brand halo of experience: For example, "From a star rating of 1-5, 5 being best, how likely would you be to using this tool / service?" 

You will not be graded on the quality of your questions, only that you have completed this step.

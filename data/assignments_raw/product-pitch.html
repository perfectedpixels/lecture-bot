Agentic AI represents a fundamental shift from traditional interfaces. An agent is a software-based entity that can perceive, reason, and act on our behalf to help achieve specific goals. Unlike smart features, agents behave - they take initiative, adapt to context, and build trust through transparency. Today's AI agents leverage large language models to perceive context, reason through problems, and take action using tools and APIs - representing a return to the original promise of autonomous, goal-driven assistive software entities.

Produce a pitch for your course project and pick a team name/business/app name that leverages Agentic AI. The concept can be any software idea that is based on existing technologies and can be replicated through a prototype which you will work on for the remainder of the quarter. It can be mobile-only app or web-based but MUST leverage Agentic AI or Generative LLMs as part of the value for the customer experience. For each bullet point, start with one single sentence to encapsulate the answer to the question, and follow up with further details by writing at least one short paragraph for each of the following:

  • Who is your core customer? Describe what makes them unique to this opportunit and what are needs specific to them. 
  • What is the customer problem or opportunity? Explain what your customer would say as the main issue they are looking to solve. 
  • Why is it important to your customer? Describe the most significant customer benefit that your concept intends to delight them with.
  • How do you envision it working? Describe the value of the user experience. Think of what activities the product or service supports, and the workflow to best accomplish the customer's goals. When answering how, think of the way your customer will move from one activity to another.

Make sure you are not singling out simply a feature, but instead representing the product vision by summarizing the above bullet points. Download here the Product Pitch exercise we did in class. 

Your concept:

  • May be mobile, web-based, or cross-platform

  • Must be based on existing or plausible near-term technology

  • Must meaningfully leverage Agentic AI and/or Generative LLMs

  • Must be feasible to prototype and iterate throughout the quarter

⚠️ This is not a feature pitch. You are proposing a product vision centered on how an AI agent collaborates with users to accomplish goals.

Extra Credit

You can boost your grade an extra credit point to your assignment, by completing a PRFAQ document. A PRFAQ is an Amazon mechanism to think about a product from customer perspective, by pitching a product concept as a press release. It is a one page narrative to imagine how a customer feels and how they may react to your idea to assure we are building a common idea behind a product vision. 

Refer here for how-to articles from Ian McAllister and Wendy Lynn-McClean to learn more about how PRFAQs work, and how to write one up. For credit, PRFAQs must be to the point, describing who is the customer, what is the problem they face today, and what is the solution FROM THE CUSTOMER'S PERSPECTIVE, and be no more than one page. You are only required to provide the Press Release portion and not the Frequently Asked Questions portion.

Specifically:

  1. Headline and subhead
  2. Summary & Date
  3. Opportunity/Problem
  4. Approach/Solution
  5. Leader Quote
  6. Description of the Customer Experience
  7. A mock customer testimonial
  8. Call to action

Customer Journey Map & Product Experience Storyboard This assignment asks you to use your existing UX frameworks to map an end-to-end customer experience, with clear attention to how AI assists the user at key moments. Using your approved persona(s), Working Backwards Pitch, and documented user goals and pain points, you will create a Customer Journey Map and a Product Experience Storyboard that show how your product—and its AI-assisted features—improves the experience over time. This is not an exercise in starting from scratch. Your work should build directly on artifacts you have already created and test whether your product concept holds up when mapped across real user touchpoints, decisions, and emotional states. You should be explicit about where AI appears in the experience, what it helps the user do, and where the user remains in control. As a group, begin by revisiting the following required inputs: Your primary persona(s) (from Assignment 2) Your Working Backwards Pitch / Product Vision Documented user goals, motivations, and pain points Any assumptions or open questions identified in earlier assignments Using these inputs, map all customer touchpoints and decision points before, during, and after the UX you plan to create. For each touchpoint, document the Pain Point, Opportunity, and Solution, noting when AI supports the user through guidance, recommendations, or automation.

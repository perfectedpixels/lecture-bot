Your wireframes should demonstrate the following class tenets, derived from our shared UX frameworks: Easy Discoverability Users can immediately understand where to start. Quick Findability Key actions and information are surfaced without cognitive searching. Cognitive Recognition Labels, patterns, and interactions align with established user mental models. Continuity Screens connect logically across time, state, and task progression. Intuitive Simplicity The experience is as simple as possible while still accomplishing the user goal. Natural Feedback User actions produce clear, expected responses. These tenets should be visible in the wireframes themselves—not just described. In-Class Walkthrough You will present a 10-minute walkthrough of your wireframes. Be prepared to: Walk through your primary use cases Explain how key screens map back to your frameworks Call out where and why the wireframes improve upon your paper prototype Wireframe Examples: wireframe example 1.pdf wireframe example 2.pdf Wireframe Example 3.pdf Rubric 4.0 — Excellent 3.0 — Good 2.0 — Marginal 1.0 — Unsatisfactory Wireframes clearly operationalize the indexed UX frameworks and product objective; the AI agent plays a meaningful, non-decorative role that reduces user effort or improves continuity; primary use cases are fully clickable end-to-end (including clear human ↔ agent interactions); the system is consistent and intuitive; class tenets (discoverability, findability, recognition, continuity, simplicity, feedback) are strongly embodied; and fidelity is appropriately low with clear improvement over the paper prototype. Framework alignment and agent presence are evident but uneven; core flows are mostly clickable with minor gaps; the system is generally understandable but has small inconsistencies; tenets are addressed but not fully reinforced; fidelity is mostly appropriate. Partial or superficial application of frameworks and AI agent value; limited or confusing click-through flows; noticeable inconsistencies; weak embodiment of UX tenets; fidelity choices distract from interaction logic. Minimal alignment to frameworks; unclear or absent AI agent value; nonfunctional or missing click-through flows; poor system coherence; tenets largely unmet; fidelity obscures intent.

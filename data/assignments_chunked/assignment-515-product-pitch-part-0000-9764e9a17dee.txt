Agentic AI represents a fundamental shift from traditional interfaces. An agent is a software-based entity that can perceive, reason, and act on our behalf to help achieve specific goals. Unlike smart features, agents behave - they take initiative, adapt to context, and build trust through transparency. Today's AI agents leverage large language models to perceive context, reason through problems, and take action using tools and APIs - representing a return to the original promise of autonomous, goal-driven assistive software entities. Produce a pitch for your course project and pick a team name/business/app name that leverages Agentic AI. The concept can be any software idea that is based on existing technologies and can be replicated through a prototype which you will work on for the remainder of the quarter. It can be mobile-only app or web-based but MUST leverage Agentic AI or Generative LLMs as part of the value for the customer experience. For each bullet point, start with one single sentence to encapsulate the answer to the question, and follow up with further details by writing at least one short paragraph for each of the following: Who is your core customer? Describe what makes them unique to this opportunit and what are needs specific to them. What is the customer problem or opportunity? Explain what your customer would say as the main issue they are looking to solve. Why is it important to your customer? Describe the most significant customer benefit that your concept intends to delight them with. How do you envision it working? Describe the value of the user experience. Think of what activities the product or service supports, and the workflow to best accomplish the customer's goals. When answering how, think of the way your customer will move from one activity to another.

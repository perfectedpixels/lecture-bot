principles (drawn from lectures and readings) to guide analysis: Balance & Harmony – Visual weight and rhythm create stability. Hierarchy & Emphasis – Relative size, color, and contrast establish order. Proportion & Scale – Relationships between elements feel intentional and pleasing. Making sure you are considering from our lecture, the principles of good design: Appealing through a positive experience (there is clarity of purpose and the elements of the design delight) Consistency of connection to its brand (elements support brand system) Follows the rules behind neuroesthetics (lack of clutter, easy to understand, pleasing use of imagery and color) Using Gestalt principles (the eye is guided to core messages or calls to action) Good use of white space (shows a focus of content or action by providing enough space around it) Appropriate and compelling content (elements of the page drive towards user goals and imagery evokes a positive association with the brand) Deft use of Size, Contrast, and Position (various content or calls to action have a hierarchy to their importance and are not competing for attention) Color Theory (utilizing to evoke an emotion value with your audience) > Aesthetic Principles Sample Assignment Try to use an analytical tone to your comments so they do not read as biased.

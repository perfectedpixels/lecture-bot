Constraint is a feature, not a limitation Unconstrained agents are irresponsible agents. Agents must explicitly refuse, redirect, or escalate when outside scope. “The model decided on its own” is not an acceptable explanation. Safety, privacy, and ethical boundaries must be designed, not assumed. 5. Failure is a design signal Initial AI outputs will be wrong. This is expected. Designers are responsible for shaping outcomes through iteration. Testing, failure analysis, and refinement are core deliverables. A system that cannot fail safely is not ready to exist. 6. The user must remain connected to the human experience AI should reduce friction, not agency. Agents should reinforce user understanding, confidence, and control. Emotional dependency, over-trust, or false authority are design risks. UX quality includes what the agentdoes not What we are listening for: Does the agent’s behavior clearly derive from the user persona and journey? Are different journey moments treated with different levels of caution and control? Is the agent’s value proposition aligned with user goals, not technical capability?

[Course: COMMLD-512]
[Concepts: heuristics, usability, user-research, ai-design]

Readable, replicable script using proper moderator language and closure. Tips on what to avoid: Leading participants (“Click on Amazon Fresh and tell me…”). Failing to list measurable success criteria. Asking compound or yes-bias questions. Overly specific tasks that test navigation, not comprehension. Neglecting satisfaction metrics or follow-up evaluation.

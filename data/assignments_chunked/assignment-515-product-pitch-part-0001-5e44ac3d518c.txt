A PRFAQ is an Amazon mechanism to think about a product from customer perspective, by pitching a product concept as a press release. It is a one page narrative to imagine how a customer feels and how they may react to your idea to assure we are building a common idea behind a product vision. Refer here for how-to articles from Ian McAllister and Wendy Lynn-McClean to learn more about how PRFAQs work, and how to write one up. For credit, PRFAQs must be to the point, describing who is the customer, what is the problem they face today, and what is the solution FROM THE CUSTOMER'S PERSPECTIVE, and be no more than one page. You are only required to provide the Press Release portion and not the Frequently Asked Questions portion.
